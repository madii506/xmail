[08]
FAQ

    

      
Is this X?
No. Xmail is unofficial and not affiliated with X Corp. or anyone who works there.

      
Who can post?
Anyone with a Solana wallet. Floors are self-declared and nobody is verified.

      
Can anyone see my wallet?
No. Mail shows your codename and floor only. The wallet signs you in and is never shown.

      
What does signing in cost?
Nothing. One signed message. It is not a transaction and cannot move anything.

      
What gets removed?
Three reports hide a mail or reply. Emails and phone numbers are redacted, slurs are scrubbed and links stay plain text.

      
Can I delete my mail?
Yes. Open it and hit delete. Drafts live only in your browser.

      
What is Pulse?
Three anonymous questions a week. One answer per wallet, counted by floor, reset every Monday.

      
Shortcuts?
c compose · / search · j k move · r reply · s star · esc close
